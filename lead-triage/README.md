# AI Lead Triage & Routing System

## Problem
When leads come in through a form, they often all land in one inbox with no prioritization — sales teams waste time manually reading and sorting every lead to figure out who should follow up and how urgently.

## What it does
This n8n workflow automatically:
1. Receives a new lead (e.g. from a form submission)
2. Sends the lead's details to an AI model to read and evaluate
3. The AI scores/categorizes the lead (e.g. by quality, intent, or type)
4. Routes the lead to the right person, team, or channel based on that category

## Who it's for
Sales teams, agencies, or any business that gets inbound leads and wants faster, more consistent triage instead of manual sorting.
